# Code of conduct

Ostadix welcomes technical disagreement and expects it to remain precise,
respectful, and directed at the work.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors with respect regardless of identity, experience, or role.
- Critique designs, evidence, and claims without attacking people.
- State uncertainty, failed checks, conflicts of interest, and evidence limits.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and requests to stop unwanted
  contact.
- Help keep discussions accessible, relevant, and technically reproducible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, sustained disruption, retaliation, plagiarism, and
publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Security
details must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Repository owner
[@lostadi](https://github.com/lostadi) is responsible for project moderation.

For platform abuse or an immediate safety concern, use GitHub's platform
reporting controls. For a sensitive project-conduct concern, use a private
channel already available to you with the maintainer. If no private channel is
available and it is safe to do so, open a minimal issue requesting private
follow-up without including sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content; lock discussions; issue a warning;
temporarily restrict participation; or permanently ban participation. The
response will be proportionate to severity, pattern, safety, and project scope.
Good-faith reports will not be punished, though knowingly false or retaliatory
reports may themselves violate this policy.
